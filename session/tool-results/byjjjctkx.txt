+There are **two suites**, and they are not run at the same time or by the same thing.
+
+| Suite | What it is | When it runs |
+|---|---|---|
+| **machinery** (`tests/`) | Pure functions over data files. No warehouse, no network, no binary. | Every commit, by the pre-commit gate and by CI. |
+| **checks** (`checks/`) | Assertions about what the built warehouse actually contains. | After `adss build`, by CI and on demand. |
+
+The split is not fastidiousness. The warehouse is not committed, so a data check in the gate
+would fail in every fresh checkout — and §6's own rules forbid both of the ways out of that (a
+skip, or a stub to assert against). Naming the two suites is what lets "every commit is green"
+stay true.
+
 | Rule | Why | Severity |
